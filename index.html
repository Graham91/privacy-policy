Privacy Policy

1. No Data Collection
We believe your private thoughts should stay completely private. This application does not collect, track, store, or share any personal data, identifiers, text logs, or user analytics.

2. Local Device Storage Only
Any content, data arrays, negative beliefs, or solutions you enter while using the application are stored exclusively on your physical device’s local memory. No data is ever transmitted over the internet to an external database, developer server, or third-party service provider.

3. Changes to This Policy
If our data storage methods ever change, this document will be updated. However, our commitment to keeping your baseline personal data secure on-device will remain our core philosophy.
